# the-paper-network (post assets)

Figures and small data extracts for the post `posts/the-paper-network.html`. The model, the full data and the app live in
https://github.com/justfetz/the-paper-network; the numbers here are copied from its October 2026 run (`data/paper_network.json`).

- `shots/*.png`: one screenshot per tab of the app, light theme, plus the chat drawer.
- `knockouts.json`: the twenty single-mill outages and their yearly cost to the network.
- `haul.json`: tons by distance band, truck and rail.
- `scenario_kpis.json`: KPIs of the footprint, counterfactual and diesel scenarios.
- `toy.json`: the three-mill, four-market instance the post animates with successive shortest paths.
