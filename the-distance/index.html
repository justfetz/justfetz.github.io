Point at a column, a line in the fan or a square to read that fight. With the chart focused, the arrow keys step through all 59. “On the calendar” moves every fight to its real date.

Notes and sources

The record, with the round and time each fight ended, comes from the professional boxing record table on Wikipedia; results, methods and venues were checked against City A.M.’s copy of the record, which agrees on every fight but one (it lists Larry Holmes as a KO, not a TKO). Totals reconcile to the official line: 59 fights, 50 wins (44 by knockout), 7 losses, 2 no contests. Checked November 2026.

Ring time is the time fought: completed rounds plus the time of the stoppage, with the minute between rounds left out. Every fight is counted at three-minute rounds except the last, against Jake Paul, which was eight two-minute rounds. Andrew Golota quit on his stool before round three in 2000, as ESPN reported at the time, so that fight is counted as two rounds even though record tables list it as a third-round retirement. A corner retirement counts as a knockout, as it does in the official record. Las Vegas includes venues in Paradise and Winchester, Nevada.
