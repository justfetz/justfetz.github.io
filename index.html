Focus

Combinatorial optimization. Scheduling, packing, cutting stock, network flow. Exact where it's tractable, heuristic where it isn't, honest about which is which.

AI for operations. Agents and retrieval on top of deterministic solvers — the model explains and proposes; the solver decides feasibility.

Trustworthy interfaces. Decision tools people will use on a plant floor: constrained inputs, visible assumptions, auditable outputs.

Security and systems. Local-first deployments and least-privilege tooling — the plumbing that makes AI safe to run near production.
