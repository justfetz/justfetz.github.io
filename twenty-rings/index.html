Notes and sources

Every regular-season and playoff game Kobe Bryant played for the Lakers is here: 1,346 and 220. The box scores, including the points by quarter, come from the open nba-datasets collection by Gonzalo Gigena (MIT license), which is compiled from Basketball-Reference. Every season line was checked against the totals on his Basketball-Reference page for games, points, shots, free throws, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks and turnovers, and they match. Minutes differ by eight in one season (2000–01), and one 1997 game has quarter scores that add up to a point more than its total. Checked November 2026.

Each ring runs from October 25 to June 20, so a spike’s position is its date; the summer gap is narrower than the calendar would make it. The split by number is computed from the games: 16,866 points in No. 8 and 16,777 in No. 24. Some published accounts give those two figures the other way round. The stories come from ESPN’s account of the day at Rucker Park, the Lakers’ note on April 12, 2013, NBC Los Angeles on the 1997 airballs, and Wikipedia for honors and dates.
