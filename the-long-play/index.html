Notes and sources

Certifications come from the RIAA Gold & Platinum database. Release dates, track listings, credited features, first-week figures (as reported by Billboard and Luminate) and Grammy results come from each album's Wikipedia entry. Review scores are from Metacritic, which does not cover the first two albums. Checked November 2026.

A record's area is its RIAA certification, which counts shipments and, for recent awards, streams; it is not the same thing as copies sold. The Death of Slim Shady has no certification yet and is drawn at one million units with a dashed edge. Infinite was a local pressing of about 1,000 copies and is shown through a lens. Curtain Call: The Hits is a compilation, so it appears only as an outline and is left out of every count.
